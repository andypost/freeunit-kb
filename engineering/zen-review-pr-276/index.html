What it fixes / logic

Title: "close the listening socket before acknowledging its removal".

Before (bug): nxt_router_listen_socket_close_ready() — the config confirmation — ran right after accept(2) was disarmed, BEFORE the FD was actually closed. That reported success ("port free") while the socket was still bound → a config re-adding the same address hit EADDRINUSE at bind time.

Now (two-phase, split by in-flight):

  • lev->count > 1 (connections in flight) → ack in phase 1 (early), because

drain timing is unpredictable and the config must not wait for idle clients.

  • lev->count == 1 (nothing in flight) → ack in phase 2, strictly AFTER

nxt_router_listen_socket_release() (the actual close). close_ready() moved from before to after the release in close_finish().

Semantics: "port free" now honestly means "FD closed" for empty listeners, and "drain started" for busy ones — not two readings of one cut.

Verdict

  • ✅ Logic correct: ack after close; two-phase split by in-flight; close_ready

in the right branch (phase1 when draining, phase2 after release).

  • ✅ Documented: the new comment states WHY the order (ack after release).
  • ✅ Removal contract preserved for empty listeners (succ. reconfig = port free).
  • ⚠️ Known limitation (honest, NOT hidden): for BUSY listeners the FD stays

bound until drain finishes → re-adding the same address can EADDRINUSE (retryable). Author flagged as follow-up ("full-drain follow-up"), did NOT close it as solved. This is the ls->count/nxt_process_quit() contract — not a bug of this PR.
