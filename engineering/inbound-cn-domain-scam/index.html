Inbound CN domain-registration scam ("freeunit" keyword pitch)

What arrived

Unsolicited email to FreeUnit (2026-09-28) from "Frank Lee (Mr.), Services & Operating Manager of a Network Service Company ... domain name registration center". Claims:

  • an application was received from "Zhenyi Holdings Ltd" to register freeunit as its

"internet keyword" plus freeunit.cn, freeunit.com.cn, freeunit.net.cn, freeunit.org.cn;

  • this "conflicts with your company name or trademark", so please confirm whether FreeUnit

is affiliated with that CN company.

  • Address given: Room 808, No. 818 Yulin Road, Shanghai 200082. T: 0086-134-8281-9147,

Tel: 0086-21-6191-8696, Fax: 0086-21-6191-8697.

Verdict: scam template, not a real registrar, not a real applicant

This is the long-documented "Chinese domain name registration scam" / slamming scheme (in the wild since ~2005, still active in 2026). Nothing about it is FreeUnit-specific.

Tell-tale signs, all present:

SignalObservation
Reused phone/fax+86-21-6191-8696 / -8697 and mobile 0086-134-8281-9147 appear across mass campaigns by the same template ("YGCHINA", "China Registry", "Domain Registry China")

Hard facts checked (whois, CNNIC, 2026-09-28)

  • freeunit.cn — already registered since 2017-01-06, registrant is an unrelated Chinese

company (Xiamen; contact dwt@duoweitree.com), registrar Xiamen 35.com (ns1-4.35.net), expiry 2031-01-06, clientTransferProhibited. So nobody can "apply for freeunit.cn" — the claim is impossible on its face.

  • freeunit.com.cn — no matching record (unregistered).
  • freeunit.net.cn — no matching record (unregistered).
  • freeunit.org.cn — no matching record (unregistered).
  • "Zhenyi Holdings": no traceable company linked to this email. A generic

"ZHENYI Holdings Limited" appears in the 2022 BVI gazette, but nothing connects it to the message — the applicant name is throwaway boilerplate.

What happens if you reply

Phase 2 (2-4 days later) is a second email, supposedly from the applicant company itself, insisting the registration will proceed, followed by an offer from "Frank" to "protect"/register the names for you during a "dispute period" — at $300-$3000 per name (a .cn costs ~$10-30 at a real registrar), often with a bogus invoice for merely answering, and frequently the domains are never actually registered.

Response policy for FreeUnit

  1. Do not reply. No reply, no question, no "who are you". Replying confirms a live

address and moves us to phase 2 / fake invoicing. Mark as spam.

  1. Delete. Nothing in FreeUnit's plan depends on freeunit.*.cn.
  2. If China-facing hosting ever matters, register through a CNNIC-accredited registrar

(e.g. Alibaba Cloud, Namecheap/Gandi for .cn resale) at normal rates — never through an unsolicited contact.

  1. Optional cheap defensive move if we ever want the orphans: freeunit.com.cn,

freeunit.net.cn, freeunit.org.cn are currently free and cost single-digit USD/yr each. Not required: FreeUnit does not trade in China, and freeunit.cn cannot be obtained anyway (held to 2031).

  1. Technical hygiene only if a real lookalike ever appears: SPF/DKIM/DMARC on

freeunit.org, registrar lock on core domains, and monitoring of certificate-transparency logs for freeunit* lookalikes.
